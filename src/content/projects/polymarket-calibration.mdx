## What I'm doing

If a Polymarket contract trades at 70 cents, the market is saying the event
has roughly a 70% chance of happening. I want to know if that's true in
practice. Across every resolved yes/no market, do the 70-cent outcomes
actually happen about 70% of the time? I'm also curious whether crypto
markets are priced any better or worse than politics, sports and the rest.

For each resolved market I take the last trade price a week, a day and an
hour before it closes. The data is a public dump of 1,266,885,846 on-chain
trades across about 4 million markets. That's around 46 GB, so instead of
downloading it I have DuckDB query it in place.
